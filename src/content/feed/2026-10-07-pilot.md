Initiation, Induction.... whatever you wanna call it, first activity post here ... yayy!

I had this idea in my head for a long time now and just wanted to build an activity feed, just to put out whatever I have on my mind. Posts are supposed to be spontaneous, unfiltered and unedited just raw thoughts whenever I have time.

I don't remember why I decided on this format exactly but I had a few sources of motivation, first one was from playing the Spider Man game that came out in 2018, I vaguely remember that game having an activity feed of sorts, that was more like Twitter in my opinion where NYC residents used to tweet stuff. I obviously don't want this to be like Twitter cause you know Twitter is shite.

The other one that I can think of is just to create a collage of raw memories that I can look back at in a few years, given that I use this thing often and maintain it properly. I saw this at my friend's (@x3ero0) place the first time where he showed us his collection of digital media and a collage of memories in the form of images, I just decided to take this online and polish it a little bit.

The last bit of motivation came from just posting stuff online, now obviously I have a blog that I've completely forgotten about lol. tbh I do want to write blogs but I just feel like the blog page contains some of my best work and I want it to hold it to that high standard always, so post less but make sure the quality is always top notch. Because each blog goes through a rigorous revision and polishing cycle it's fucking exhausting to write one these days. So this place is perfect because I won't even bother checking for spelling mistakes here, whatever lands on my keyboard goes straight up here. Also proves as a good outlet for me to just unload things here and just sort of unwind if I've no one to talk to.

Though I still need to make a some architectural decisions as to where I wanna store these little posts, because a CMS is overkill for this and I don't like storing personal things on github. I'm currently thinking of a few ideas to setup my home server but until then I guess this iswhere we're at.

So here's my little yap session, hopefully many more to come...
