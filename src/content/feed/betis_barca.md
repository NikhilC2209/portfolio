Feel bad for my guy, just when I thought Rodri's header would be enough to help us maintain a lead until the halftime mark Lo Celso scored an incomprehensible goal. But after a lengthy VAR check it was ruled out for handball, rightfully so tbh but even as a Barca fan I kinda wanted the goal to stand.

Out of the box, first time volley, on the turn and straight into the top corner, nothing Joan Garcia could do there. Reminds me of that goal Egpyt scored vs Argentina which was ruled out as well, the only difference here is that Betis didn't have full control of the ball after the build up whereas I think Egpyt never lost the ball. Nevertheless, sad to see a Goal of the month goal get ruled out.
