What an exciting group stage game by the two, national football between two top teams just hits different even when there's nothing on the line. Having said that England just ended Spain's 43 game unbeaten streak (the longest in history btw) with a top performance. 

But Tuchel really needs to control these games better, being 3-0 up and just barely winning 3-2 is not the standard for the English team, they need to defend better and see the game off more comfortably. Losing games like these is what's keeping them from an international trophy. If not for the amazing double save at the end by James Trafford, they would've lost this game as well, but props to Trafford for showing up in such a high profile fixture.
